About Team Speed Studios

A four-person software team with real names, a real client and a working product.

Why we exist

Manual-handling induction is usually a poster, a PDF and a forgettable session. Staff sit through it and then lift loads without having practised a single decision. We formed Team Speed Studios to build training that people have to think their way through.

Lift Right began as a real client brief for the CET257 Enterprise Project at University of Sunderland (ISMT College). We are a student team, and we say so openly: what you see on this site is what we have actually built.

Our mission

To make workplace manual-handling training something people actually remember, by letting them practise the decisions rather than just reading about them.


Our tagline

Learn safe handling by doing it.

What we stand for

Four principles that shape every decision we make.
